BOOKMARK CLEANER
Finds and deletes dead links in your bookmarks.
Works with Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Firefox, and exported bookmark .html files.


OPENING IT

Windows: Double-click "Bookmark Cleaner.exe".
         If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
         then "Run anyway". This only happens the first time.

Mac:     The first time, RIGHT-click "Bookmark Cleaner" and choose "Open",
         then click "Open" again. (Double-clicking the first time gives a
         warning because the app isn't registered with Apple.)
         If it still refuses: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
         down, and click "Open Anyway".

Linux:   Double-click "Bookmark Cleaner". If nothing happens, right-click it,
         open Properties, and allow it to run as a program.


USING IT

1. Pick your browser from the list, or click "Browse for a file" to choose
   an exported bookmarks .html file.
2. Click "Check links". Large collections can take a few minutes.
3. Review the list:
     Dead   = the page or website is gone. Ticked for deletion automatically.
     Unsure = the site blocked the check, was slow, or had a temporary error.
              These usually still work, so they're NOT ticked.
              Double-click a row to open the link and see for yourself.
   Click the box in the Delete column to tick or untick a link.
4. CLOSE YOUR BROWSER completely, then click "Delete ticked bookmarks".
   A backup copy of your bookmarks is saved first.


GOOD TO KNOW

- If you sync bookmarks across devices (signed in to Chrome/Edge/Firefox),
  turn bookmark sync off before deleting, or the links may come back from
  your other devices. Turn it back on afterwards.
- Browsers can keep running in the background after you close the window.
  On Windows check the icons near the clock; on a Mac use Quit from the menu.
- To undo a clean-up: close the browser, go to the folder shown in the final
  message, delete the bookmarks file, and rename the ".backup-..." file to the
  original name.
